# CREATING A NEW SITE SKIN

You will need an AO3 account and must be logged in to install this site skin.

1. Choose your preferred version of The Midnight Library from the links above.
2. Open the corresponding CSS file and copy its entire contents using the copy button on GitHub.
3. Log into your AO3 account and click on your username at the top of the page.
4. Select **Dashboard** from the dropdown menu.
5. Go to **Skins** in your Dashboard.
6. Click on **Create Site Skin**.
7. Give your skin a unique title. You can add your username to "The Midnight Library" to make sure the title is not already in use.
8. Paste the copied code into the **CSS** box.
9. Scroll to the bottom of the page and click **Submit**.
10. Once your skin has been saved, click **Use** to activate it.
