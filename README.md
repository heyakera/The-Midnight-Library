
# The Midnight Library

A dark academia-inspired AO3 skin with a variety of gold ornaments <3

# SKIN FILES

The Midnight Library consists of three complementary CSS files:

- [Combined CSS](The-Midnight-Library-Combined.css)
- [PC CSS](The-Midnight-Library-PC.css)
- [Mobile CSS](The-Midnight-Library-Mobile.css)

!!! All three skins are designed to work together to provide a complete experience across desktop and mobile devices !!!

# CREATING A NEW SITE SKIN

In order to install this site skin, you will need an AO3 account and must be logged in.

## STEP 1 — Creating the PC Skin

1. Open the **PC CSS** file linked above and copy the entire code using the copy button on GitHub.
2. Log into your AO3 account and click on your username at the top of the page.
3. Select **Dashboard** from the dropdown menu.
4. Go to **Skins**.
5. Click on **Create Site Skin**.
6. Give your skin a unique title, such as `The Midnight Library - PC - YourUsername`.
7. Paste the copied code into the **CSS** box.
8. Leave the Advanced settings unchanged.
9. Scroll to the bottom of the page and click **Submit**.

Do not click **Use** yet.

## STEP 2 — Creating the Mobile Skin

1. Open the **Mobile CSS** file linked above and copy the entire code.
2. Create another site skin.
3. Give your skin a unique title, such as `The Midnight Library - Mobile - YourUsername`.
4. Paste the copied code into the **CSS** box.
5. Find the **Advanced** section and click **Show**.
6. Under **Media**, select `only screen and (max-width: 42em)`.
7. Leave the remaining Advanced settings unchanged.
8. Scroll to the bottom of the page and click **Submit**.

Do not click **Use** yet.

## STEP 3 — Creating the Combined Skin

1. Open the **Combined CSS** file linked above and copy the entire code.
2. Create another site skin.
3. Give your skin a unique title, such as `The Midnight Library - Combined - YourUsername`.
4. Paste the copied code into the **CSS** box.
5. Find the **Advanced** section and click **Show**.
6. Leave the **Media** settings unchanged.
7. Locate the **Parent Skins** section.
8. Add your PC skin as the first parent skin.
9. Click **Add parent skin** and add your Mobile skin as the second parent skin.
10. Make sure the PC skin is listed first and the Mobile skin second.
11. Scroll to the bottom of the page and click **Submit**.

## STEP 4 — Activating the Skin

Once all three skins have been created and linked, open your **Combined** skin and click **Use**.

!!! You do not need to activate the PC or Mobile skins separately. The Combined skin will automatically apply the appropriate styling according to your screen size !!!
